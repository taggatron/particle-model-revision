# GCSE Physics Revision Grid: Matter, Heat, Pressure & Density

An interactive, responsive 4x4 revision flashcard grid with point-based recall-to-challenge questions and Green Pen Marking answers, designed for GCSE Combined Science and Physics (Foundation Tier).

## 4x4 Question Grid & Points Structure

Each row covers a core physics topic with graded difficulty:
- **1 Point (Recall):** Direct factual recall
- **2 Points (Describe):** Clear scientific description
- **3 Points (Apply):** Applying knowledge to real-world scenarios or calculations
- **4 Points (Challenge):** Explanations requiring multiple concepts or multi-step reasoning

### Row 1: States of Matter & Changes
- **Q1 (1 pt • Recall):** What do we call the change of state when solid ice turns into liquid water?
  - *Answer:* Melting.
- **Q2 (2 pts • Describe):** How are particles arranged in a solid?
  - *Answer:* Tightly packed together in neat/regular rows (vibrating on the spot).
- **Q3 (3 pts • Apply):** Steam hits a cold mirror and turns into water droplets. What is the name of this state change?
  - *Answer:* Condensing (or condensation).
- **Q4 (4 pts • Challenge):** Why are changes of state called physical changes rather than chemical changes?
  - *Answer:* Because they are easy to reverse and no new substance is made.

### Row 2: Specific Heat & Latent Heat
- **Q5 (1 pt • Recall):** Which heats up faster: water or metal?
  - *Answer:* Metal.
- **Q6 (2 pts • Describe):** What happens to the temperature of ice while it is melting into water?
  - *Answer:* It stays the same (stays flat at 0°C).
- **Q7 (3 pts • Apply):** Is the energy needed to melt a solid without changing its temperature called specific or latent heat?
  - *Answer:* Latent heat.
- **Q8 (4 pts • Challenge):** At the beach, why does the sea stay cool while the sand gets very hot on a sunny day?
  - *Answer:* Water has a much higher specific heat capacity than sand, so it takes much more energy to warm up.

### Row 3: Gas Pressure
- **Q9 (1 pt • Recall):** True or False: Gas particles stay completely still inside a balloon.
  - *Answer:* False (they move very fast in all directions).
- **Q10 (2 pts • Describe):** What causes gas pressure inside a container?
  - *Answer:* Gas particles bumping/colliding into the walls of the container.
- **Q11 (3 pts • Apply):** What happens to the speed of gas particles when you heat them up?
  - *Answer:* They move faster (gain kinetic energy).
- **Q12 (4 pts • Challenge):** Why does pumping more air into a bike tyre increase the pressure inside it?
  - *Answer:* There are more particles inside, so they hit the walls more often.

### Row 4: Density & Floating
- **Q13 (1 pt • Recall):** What do we call the upward push of water that helps boats float?
  - *Answer:* Upthrust.
- **Q14 (2 pts • Describe):** Water has a density of 1.0 g/cm³. Will an ice cube (density 0.9 g/cm³) float or sink?
  - *Answer:* Float (because 0.9 is less dense than 1.0).
- **Q15 (3 pts • Apply):** What is the formula for calculating Density using Mass and Volume?
  - *Answer:* Density = Mass ÷ Volume.
- **Q16 (4 pts • Challenge):** Calculate the density of a rock with a Mass of 30 g and a Volume of 6 cm³.
  - *Answer:* 30 ÷ 6 = 5 g/cm³.

## Features

- **40 Total Points Scheme:** Real-time point score counter and card tracker.
- **Interactive 3D Flashcards:** Click to flip, smooth 3D flip animation, hover lift effects.
- **Row Filtering:** Filter to view individual rows (10 pts each) or all 16 cards (40 pts).
- **Green Pen Marking:** Clear, concise answers for quick self-assessment or peer marking.
- **Flip All & Reset All:** Fast controls for classroom quizzes or revision testing.
- **Fully Responsive:** Adapts cleanly from 4-column desktop to mobile devices.
- **Pure Web Technologies:** Zero external dependencies (Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JS).
